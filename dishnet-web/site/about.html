What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide reliable, affordable, and innovative enterprise and consumer communications across Sudan and beyond — connecting remote locations where no fiber or cell coverage exists, and empowering communities with the tools to thrive in the digital age.

Our Vision

To be East Africa's most trusted connectivity partner — bridging the digital divide across Sudan by making world-class internet accessible to every home, business, and organization regardless of location.

Our Journey

12+ Years of Innovation

From a small VSAT provider to Sudan's leading multi-technology ISP.

July 2013

DishNet Africa Founded

Established as a licensed ISP in East Africa, starting with VSAT satellite internet services for businesses and NGOs.

2014–2016

National Expansion

Expanded to nationwide operations in our first East African market. Deployed Ka-Band, Ku-Band, and C-Band satellite platforms, carrying over 180 Mbps across networks.

2017–2019

Enterprise Growth

Became a major connectivity provider for international NGOs, UN agencies, and government offices. Introduced managed IT services, structured cabling, and business WiFi solutions.

2020–2022

Fiber Network Build-Out

Began deploying fiber-optic infrastructure and launched a Fiber-to-the-Home (FTTH) broadband service with speeds up to 60 Mbps.

2023–2024

Starlink Partnership

Began supplying and installing Starlink. Introduced private LTE/4G services for enterprise customers.

2025

Multi-Technology ISP

Now operating across Starlink satellite, fiber, LTE, and traditional VSAT — delivering connectivity solutions for every need and budget across the country.
