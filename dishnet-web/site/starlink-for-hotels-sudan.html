For hotels & guesthouses

Guest WiFi that actually works

Guests judge a hotel by its WiFi within the first hour. One dish, sized honestly to your rooms, changes that review.

Ask about your property on WhatsApp See the kits

Sizing by occupancy, not optimism

Guests stream. A small guesthouse fits 2TB; larger properties with full rooms belong at 3TB or above. When guests pass the priority allowance the connection continues on unlimited standard data — busy months degrade gracefully instead of cutting off.

One dish, a whole property

The Gen 3 router covers up to 297 m² and 235 devices — a strong start for a compact property. Larger buildings need additional access points wired from the router’s two Ethernet ports; tell us the layout on WhatsApp and we will spec it with you.

The arithmetic for a property

One-time: kit ($600 Standard) + $50 installation. Monthly: the plan. All of it on one page: prices.
