Starlink currently sells two consumer kits, and we supply both. The Standard is the kit for homes and offices — current-generation kickstand dish, WiFi 6 router, sealed against dust. The Mini is the size of a laptop, runs on 25–40 W and travels in a backpack — ideal for field teams and anyone on the move. The Standard kit is $600 and the Mini is $350, one-time; professional installation is $50 one-time. Your monthly plan is charged separately. Contact us on WhatsApp for personalized advice.
Starlink is designed for self-installation. However, we offer professional installation to ensure optimal dish placement, cable routing, and signal quality — highly recommended for permanent setups and businesses.
