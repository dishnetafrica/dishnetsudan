DishNet provides Starlink satellite internet (as an official reseller), fiber-to-the-home broadband, private LTE/4G networks, VSAT satellite services, structured cabling, managed IT, and business WiFi solutions across Sudan.
DishNet has operated across Sudan since 2013 and is now bringing that experience to Sudan. Starlink works anywhere in Sudan with a clear sky view. Fiber is currently available in select areas.

Starlink is a satellite internet service by SpaceX using thousands of low-orbit satellites. It delivers high-speed, low-latency internet anywhere with a clear view of the sky — no cell towers or fiber cables needed.
Typical download speeds range from 50 to 220 Mbps with upload speeds of 5–20 Mbps. Latency is usually 20–40 ms, suitable for video calls, streaming, and online gaming.
For most homes: the Mini Kit ($299) or V4 Standard ($550). For businesses needing maximum performance: the Flat High Performance ($2,600). The Standard Actuated ($650) is great for permanent outdoor installations. Contact us on WhatsApp for personalized advice.
Starlink is designed for self-installation. However, we offer professional installation ($50) to ensure optimal dish placement, cable routing, and signal quality — highly recommended for permanent setups and businesses.
Residential ($80/mo unlimited), Priority plans from $112–$336/mo with faster peak speeds, and Roam ($65/mo for 50GB mobile use). All plans include unlimited standard data.

Three tiers: Basic (20 Mbps, $50/mo), Standard (40 Mbps, $75/mo), and Premium (60 Mbps, $100/mo). All plans include unlimited data with fair usage policy.
Installation is a one-time fee of $100. This includes running fiber to your premises, installing the ONT (Optical Network Terminal), and setting up your Wi-Fi router.
Once your area is confirmed as serviceable, installation typically takes 1–3 business days. Our technicians handle everything with minimal disruption to your property.
All fiber plans include unlimited data. The Fair Usage Policy (FUP) only applies in cases of extreme or unusual consumption that could affect other users on the network. Normal heavy use — streaming, gaming, working from home — is not affected.
