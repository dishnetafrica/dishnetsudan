Starlink Kits

Choose your Starlink

Genuine Starlink hardware, delivered anywhere in Sudan and installed by certified technicians. Professional installation is $50 one-time. Your monthly plan is separate — kits are a one-time purchase.

Portable

Starlink Mini

$350 one-time

The size of a laptop and about 1.1 kg — it travels in a backpack and is online in minutes. Built for field teams, aid work, travel between cities, and any site that moves.

  • Typically 50–150+ Mbps download
  • WiFi router built into the dish
  • Low power: 25–40 W, runs from a power bank or solar with the right cable
  • IP67 — sealed against dust and rain
Most popular

Starlink Standard

$600 one-time

The current-generation kickstand dish — no motors, nothing to jam, tougher than the old actuated design. The kit for homes, offices, guesthouses and WiFi zones.

  • Typically 100–250 Mbps download
  • WiFi 6 router — covers up to 297 m², up to 235 devices
  • IP67 dish, rated −30°C to 50°C and 96+ kph winds
  • Sealed against dust — built for Sudanese conditions
