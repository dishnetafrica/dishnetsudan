Fiber vs Starlink: Which Is Right for You?

DishNet offers both fiber broadband and Starlink satellite internet. They are both excellent — but they serve different needs. Here is how to choose.

Choose Fiber If...

  • You are in a Juba area where fiber is available
  • You want the lowest possible latency (great for gaming and video calls)
  • You want a fixed monthly cost starting at $50/month
  • You prefer a wired, always-on connection with no weather sensitivity
  • You need symmetrical upload and download speeds

Choose Starlink If...

  • You are outside Juba or in an area without fiber
  • You need internet at a remote camp, project site, or field office
  • You want portability — take your internet with you when you travel
  • You need very high download speeds (up to 220 Mbps)
  • You want internet immediately without waiting for infrastructure

Quick Comparison

Fiber: 20-60 Mbps, $50-100/month, $100 installation, Juba only, unlimited data, wired connection.

Starlink: 50-220 Mbps, $65-336/month, $299-2,600 hardware, works anywhere, satellite connection.

Can I Have Both?

Yes. Some businesses use fiber as their primary connection and keep a Starlink kit as backup for outages. This ensures you are never without internet. Contact us to design a solution that fits your needs.
