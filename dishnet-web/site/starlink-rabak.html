Starlink in Rabak

Rabak is capital of White Nile state, facing Kosti across the river, roughly 320 km south of Khartoum. Starlink needs no cable, no exchange and no local network — just a clear view of the sky.

Why satellite works in Rabak

State offices and the agricultural businesses around them need a connection that is independent of the crossing.

Because the connection comes from orbit rather than from a local exchange, the speed you get in Rabak is the same as anywhere else in White Nile State — distance from Khartoum makes no difference to it.

Rabak faces Kosti across the White Nile, and the two are often treated as one market. In practice a connection on one bank tells you nothing about service on the other.
