For rural areas

No tower. No cable. Just sky.

Rural connectivity has always waited for infrastructure to arrive. A satellite dish ends the waiting: if you can see the sky, you can be online.

Why satellite fits rural Sudan

Terrestrial networks reach towns first and villages late or never. A Starlink dish needs no tower and no cable — the signal comes from orbit, the same service in a village as in Khartoum. See the cities we are asked about most — and note the point of that page: absence from the list has never meant absence of service.

Power, solved simply

Many rural sites run on solar already. The Mini’s 25–40 W works with modest solar-and-battery setups; the Standard kit’s 75–100 W suits a household inverter. Ask us and we will size the power alongside the kit.

Ordering from far away

The whole process runs on WhatsApp — plan advice, exact prices from our billing system, and delivery arrangements confirmed per order, honestly, for your location.
