Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about our services.

Starlink is a satellite internet constellation operated by SpaceX. It uses thousands of low-orbit satellites to deliver high-speed, low-latency internet anywhere with a clear view of the sky. DishNet supplies and professionally installs Starlink in Sudan.
Typical download speeds range from 100 to 250 Mbps per Starlink’s specification sheets, with lower speeds possible in busy periods. Latency is typically well under 100 ms, which is suitable for video calls, streaming, and online gaming.
Starlink kits are designed for self-installation. However, we offer professional installation to ensure optimal dish placement, proper cable routing, and the best possible signal. This is especially recommended for business locations and permanent setups.
Our plans are priced in US dollars. Message us on WhatsApp to ask about paying in Sudanese pounds and which payment methods are available in your city.
