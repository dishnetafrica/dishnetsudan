Ultra-Portable Satellite Internet

The Starlink Mini is SpaceX's most compact terminal — small enough to fit in a backpack, powerful enough to stream 4K video from virtually anywhere on Earth. It's the ideal solution for travellers, field workers, and anyone who needs reliable internet in remote Sudanese locations where fiber and cellular coverage doesn't reach.

How It Works

Starlink uses a constellation of thousands of low-earth orbit (LEO) satellites to deliver high-speed internet. The Mini's electronically steered phased-array antenna automatically locks onto overhead satellites — you just need a clear view of the sky. There's no manual alignment, no technician required for basic setup.

Perfect For

  • Traveling professionals and digital nomads
  • Small homes and apartments

The Starlink Mini is designed for effortless self-installation — most customers are online within 15 minutes. For permanent or business setups, our professional installation service ensures optimal placement and signal quality.

1

Unbox & Power

Remove from box, unfold the kickstand, and connect the USB-C power cable to a power source.

2

Point at Sky

Place the dish where it has a clear view of the sky. Use the Starlink app to check for obstructions.

3

Connect

Join the "Starlink" Wi-Fi network from your device. The dish auto-aligns to satellites in about 5 minutes.
